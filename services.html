PT

Physical Therapy

It is a health care profession that provides treatment to individuals to develop, maintain and restore maximum movement and function throughout life. This includes providing treatment in circumstances where movement and function are threatened by aging, injury, or disease.

Physical therapy is concerned with identifying and maximizing quality of life and movement potential within the spheres of promotion, prevention, treatment/intervention, and rehabilitation.

CL

Lymphedema Therapy

Description: Specialized care for swelling and lymphedema-related limitations.

Benefits: Swelling management, comfort, mobility, and patient self-care confidence.

Process: Measurements, education, compression guidance, exercise, and follow-up planning.

Ask about lymphedema care
OR

Orthopedic Rehabilitation

Description: Recovery care for joints, muscles, spine, postural issues, and post-surgical needs.

Benefits: Strength, flexibility, joint confidence, and safer return to daily activities.

Process: Diagnosis-specific exam, exercise progression, manual therapy, and outcome tracking.

Ask about orthopedic rehab
SR

Sports Rehabilitation

Description: Rehab for athletes and active people returning to training, play, and recreation.

Benefits: Safer return to sport, improved mechanics, conditioning, and injury-risk reduction.

Process: Sport demands review, strength and agility progressions, and return-to-activity testing.

Ask about sports rehab
ON

Oncology Rehabilitation

Description: Support for oncology-related impairments, weakness, mobility changes, and fatigue.

Benefits: Better function, confidence, swelling awareness, and activity tolerance.

Process: Careful evaluation, symptom-sensitive exercise, education, and coordination as needed.

Ask about oncology rehab
